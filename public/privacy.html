DrivePool

Privacy policy

DrivePool is a private, self-hosted tool. This copy is run by one person for their own Google accounts and is not offered to the public. Access is protected by a password.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a Google account, DrivePool asks Google for access to that account's Google Drive. It uses this access only to show storage usage and to list, upload, download, rename, move and delete files when the owner asks it to.

What it stores

DrivePool stores the connected account's email address, display name and the access tokens Google issues, so the owner does not have to sign in to Google on every visit. Files and file names are not stored by DrivePool. They stay in Google Drive and are only passed through while a file is being listed or downloaded.

What it shares

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Data from Google is not used for any purpose other than the features described above.

Removing access

Choosing Disconnect next to an account deletes its stored tokens and revokes DrivePool's access at Google. Access can also be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security, in the list of third-party apps with account access.
